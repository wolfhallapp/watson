# The Construct: 1,000 small ways to earn (started 2026-10-25)

Horace's project with the Oracle: find 1,000 small, legit ways to make a little money from home,
from 15 cents a day up. The trunk is small earners; each branch is a family of strategies.

- **Where it lives:** Notion page "The Construct" (private draft, id 405f0daa-f2d8-813d-8b5c-e89dd1ef679c),
  with the database **The Tree** (data source `collection://dc4d6a46-0f86-49c3-8884-cb7e3c1884e6`).
  One row per strategy: Branch, Est $/month, Setup, Daily time, Oracle's role, Risk, Status
  (Idea / Researching / Started / Earning / Paused / Dropped), Earned to date, How it works, Watch out, Priority.
- **Branches:** 1 Passive devices · 2 Cashback & receipts · 3 Rewards & points · 4 Paid opinions & testing ·
  5 AI training work · 6 Found money · 7 Sell your stuff · 8 Digital products · 9 Services with AI ·
  10 Content & affiliate.
- **Rules:** legit and inside each site's terms (no bots where banned, one account per person, no fake reviews),
  **no gambling or sweepstakes casinos as an earning branch**, track real payouts not estimates, keep tax records.
  Agents can research, write listings, build products and find claims; work that a site requires a human to do
  (surveys, AI-training tasks, Microsoft Rewards) stays Horace's.
- **First seeds (10/25):** 23 strategies. Priority 1: unclaimed property search, EcoSport insurance refund,
  class action settlements, Fetch receipts, Microsoft Rewards, Prolific, Respondent, a Notion budget template to sell.
